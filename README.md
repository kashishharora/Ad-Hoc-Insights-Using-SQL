# AtliQ Hardware – Ad-Hoc Business Insights Using SQL
### Project Overview

AtliQ Hardware is a fast-growing computer hardware and peripherals company with a global presence. The company sells a wide range of products such as PCs, printers, mice, keyboards, storage devices, and other computer peripherals.

AtliQ sells its products through multiple channels, including retailers, distributors, direct channels, brick-and-mortar stores, and e-commerce platforms such as Amazon and Flipkart. Retail partners can include companies such as Croma and Best Buy.

As the company continued to grow, its sales volume, customers, products, and business transactions also increased significantly. The existing approach of relying heavily on Excel files became difficult to manage when management needed quick answers to specific business questions.

To overcome this challenge, the sales data was organized in a SQL database, allowing analysts to quickly retrieve and analyze large volumes of data.

The objective of this project is to answer ad-hoc business requests from management using SQL and convert the query results into meaningful business insights.
